# EU representative

GDPR Art 27 requires controllers and processors **without an establishment in the EU** to designate a representative in the EU when they offer goods or services to EU data subjects, or monitor their behaviour.

The representative is named in the Privacy Policy and is the first contact point for EU supervisory authorities and data subjects in the EU.

**Status**: not yet appointed. This is a Critical-tier gap for any EU rollout.

## Who qualifies

- A natural person or legal entity **established in an EU member state** where data subjects live.
- Authorised in writing (a contractual mandate) to represent us under GDPR.
- Not necessarily a lawyer — many GDPR-rep firms specialise in this.

## UK GDPR (Art 27) — separate appointment

Brexit means the UK has its own UK GDPR. If we offer services to UK data subjects without a UK establishment, we need a **UK representative** *in addition* to the EU one. Multiple firms (e.g. VeraSafe) act in both jurisdictions.

## What to record once appointed

Update this doc with:

- Legal name of the representative entity.
- Establishment address (must be within the EU; same for UK rep).
- Contact email + phone.
- Contract execution date.
- Renewal date.
- Liability + indemnification terms (the rep can be jointly liable with us under Art 27(5) — confirm scope).

Then update:

- The Privacy Policy footer + DSAR section.
- The Cookie Notice if it cites the controller separately.
- App Store Connect → App Privacy → Privacy Contact (Apple uses this for any EU regulator inquiry).
- Play Console → App content → Data safety → Privacy Policy URL (which itself lists the rep).
- The signature block on any DPA with EU sub-processors.

## When this matters in practice

- A **supervisory-authority inquiry** under Art 31. The SA may write to the rep instead of us. Set up automatic forwarding to `privacy@<your-domain>`.
- A **data-subject Art 12 inquiry**. Users in the EU can write to the rep instead of us; same forwarding rule.
- An **Art 33 breach notification**. We must be reachable through the rep; some SAs require the notification *from* the rep.

The rep's standing offer is the cleanest "ask counsel if unsure" answer — the rep firms specialise in this and answer this question for a living.
